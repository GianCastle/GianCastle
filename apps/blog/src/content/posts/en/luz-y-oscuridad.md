Darkness is a bottomless pit where evil and malice take hold of the cabal. Conspiring against joy and delight, with the fear and anguish they prepare. A whirlwind of nightmares, watering roots of torment, which wherever it settles, will open a void without consent.

Darkness hides in wells of ink... It conceals malice and wicked guilt. Light shines in splendor, proclaiming truth, joy, but light only thrives in the shadows and without them there is no shine or glory… or beauty.
