Over the last few weeks, everything I've seen in the news, in the newspaper, on Facebook, all over the Internet, are words of hate. I see stories of resentment toward several presidential candidates, I see bright smiles that deep down are crying out for help, I see stories full of false assumptions and a sense of entitlement. What I see, and it saddens me to admit it, is a pettiness and an immeasurable lack of respect. And it's something I have a very hard time understanding.

We are all people. Yes, we are all equal. Sure, we are individuals and we have different personalities and different life situations, but in the end, we are all people, we are earthlings of elevated consciousness, we are all human. And every human being, regardless of their race, gender, origin, social status, age, sexual orientation, religion or political party deserves to be treated with love and respect. Every person deserves to be treated with compassion. 

As people, it is our duty to love one another. When our neighbor, be it animal or human, falls, it is our job to pick them up. When they need our help, we must be there to help them; even if it sounds selfish, we should do it just because we know we would like their help if we were in the same situation. We all need each other. We need to love each other. We need to show compassion for our fellow beings. 

As people, we must respect one another. The moment we refer to someone as something that is not our equal, respect is lost. Labels, yes… those blessed labels. A moral filth we use daily to disrespect one another, a justification that deep down is empty. We need to refer to others as our fellow beings, not by their race, their sexual orientation, their age, their gender, their religion or their political affiliation, just some of the things we use to divide ourselves as a species. And although, yes, many of these things make us who we are, we should not be disrespected for the things we are made of. We must be respected regardless of our singularities. In the end…

>*What makes us great is the fact that we can see how small we are…*
**and truly realize how infinitely vast our minds can be.**

Respecting or loving one another doesn't mean we always have to agree with everything they do or say. It means we must respect that they have an opinion different from yours, it means we don't need to attack them for having an opinion different from ours.

I have a very hard time understanding why we can't behave that way, even myself in certain circumstances.

I hope that someday, when I open my laptop, I can see stories of happiness, love, peace and respect. I hope I can turn on the car radio and hear stories of people helping other people. I hope that we, as a society, as a people, as a species, are capable of showing our fellow beings respect, dignity and affection because as a species, we deserve no less.

My wish 24  for this world is to abolish hate and anger. We have wasted more than enough time and energy on disrespect and  separation, on wars and greed… precious energy and time that we could have devoted to improving the world and our society.

Maybe it's too utopian?
Perhaps...
