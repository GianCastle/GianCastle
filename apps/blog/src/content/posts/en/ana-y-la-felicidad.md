Once upon a time there was a little girl named Ana who lived in a small village surrounded by mountains. Ana was a happy girl, she was always smiling and enjoying life. She could often be found running through the fields or playing with her friends. 

For Ana, happiness was something natural and easy to reach. But as Ana grew up, she realized that not everyone was as happy as she was. Some people seemed to always be sad or worried, and Ana couldn't understand why. She knew that life could be hard sometimes, but deep down she always thought there was something good to be found in every situation.

One day, while walking through the forest, overwhelmed by an unforeseen situation, Ana came across a wise old man who lived in a small cabin in the heart of the forest. The man was sitting on his balcony, enjoying the afternoon sun, when Ana approached and asked him: "Why are some people not happy?"

The man looked at her and smiled. "Happiness is something each one of us has within ourselves", he said. "Some people simply haven't found the way to discover it yet".

Ana nodded, but she wasn't sure she completely understood what the man meant. "But, how can I be happy all the time?", she asked.

The man smiled. "You can't be happy all the time, dear. Life has ups and downs, and sometimes we need to experience sadness or pain to be able to appreciate happiness when it comes".

Ana frowned. She wasn't sure she wanted to experience sadness or pain, but she decided to listen to what the man had to say.

The man told her about the importance of gratitude and love. "There is always something to be grateful for, even in the darkest moments", he said. "And if you can love others, even those who hurt you, you will find happiness in places you never imagined".

Ana nodded, thinking of all the things she was grateful for. She had a loving family, incredible friends and a safe home to live in. But, how could she love someone who had hurt her?

The man told her about compassion and forgiveness. "When you forgive someone who hurt you, you're not doing anything for that person, but for yourself. You free yourself from the burden of resentment and anger, and that allows you to find happiness in your heart".

Ana felt overwhelmed by all of the man's wisdom. She knew it would take time to learn everything he had taught her, but she was determined to try.

Days turned into weeks and weeks into months, and Ana strove to put into practice everything she had learned from the wise man. Sometimes, it was hard. There were moments when she felt sad or angry, moments when life seemed too hard to handle.
But Ana always, always found her way back to happiness.
