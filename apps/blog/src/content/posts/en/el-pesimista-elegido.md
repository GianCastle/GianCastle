The chosen pessimist, always sees life upside down, he carries a weight on his back, that won't let him see beyond his feet.

Sadness wraps around him, like a dark cloud and although he wants to find the light, his mind grows more insecure.

It is easy to judge him for his attitude, and say he is a defeated man but his vision of the world is his virtue and sometimes more accurate than imagined.

Truth is his banner and honesty his flag, he does not deceive himself and what he says he always means.

His fears are everyone's but he faces them with courage, his heart beats without rest and his soul is always keeping vigil.

Perhaps in his pessimism, there is a constant struggle to find happiness even if the road is disheartening.

It is not easy to be a pessimist, nor to carry so much pain, but his strength is his sight and his heart full of love.

So let us not judge the chosen one, who walks through the world upside down, perhaps we do not know his suffering and the treasure he carries in his being.
