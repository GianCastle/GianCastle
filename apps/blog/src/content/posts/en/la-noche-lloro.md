The night cried, over a song that still reminds us of those times together.

That afternoon turned into a beautiful new color, and the stars shone from the sky just to soothe our fury. And in that moment, to be honest, our tears fell; forming a beautiful sea of illusions that reflected us and gave us peace for an instant, for a few moments. The sun began to fall and our love ended with it. But, we decided to go on, down a new path and start once more.

The night cried over a song that still remembers that love; I wanted to see you, touch you, be together. All I need is to be able to scoop up happiness with a small spoon. What we feel is real and in time you will remember it.

We walked along the path of loneliness. We dreamed and found the road that could truly bring us together. Don't say goodbye, I know there is hope. We can love each other, we just have to try. Let's come together again under a promise, for destiny is calling us. I have dragged this heavy burden down a long road. Forget the past, the future, everything, just think of us both. 

The rain murmured a goodbye to me in a song that remembered the lost love. I gave you the kindness found inside my lonely being. The chance that we might meet again has not vanished yet. 

In a bed you sleep, next to the stardust that surrounds us and in my loneliness I searched for the lights in a town without seasons, without summers, or mists; while every night I look at the stars to dream that you are still next to me at the peak of our trail... as complicated as it may seem, we are two children sensitive to the cold who simply gaze at the sky in different ways.

The night cried... over a song  that still remembers our lost love. I hate being alone, I wish I could see you, I wish I could feel you, I wish we were together. All I need is to be able to scoop up happiness with a spoon. You know what you feel is real, look closely and in time you will remember it; just remember that the night cried in this song, which comes from the soul... from our soul. Look again and... tell me if you will find the answer in my eyes. The tears fall on their own, just forgive, just say so, if you truly love me. Never forget the feeling we had
