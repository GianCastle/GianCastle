## What a shame

I can't escape this haunted hallway, my own ghost and everything I love and have tried to push away.

Come hell or high water, I am as miserable as sin and this position I find myself in never prevails. That's why they say,

> Speak of the devil and he will appear. The burdens are so heavy... every day it becomes more evident. You are submerged.

I try to stay strong but I always give in; wishing I could escape from myself, wishing I could push away this evil temptation.... wishing I could kill this thing I love.
