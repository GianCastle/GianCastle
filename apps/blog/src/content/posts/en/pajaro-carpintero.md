In the spot where I used to sit at the office where I work, three coworkers were working on the same project, while I was on a totally different one. And yes... I say "used to" because I'm not sitting there anymore. My work week greeted me with the news that I was going to be moved. The reason for this was that a new person joined for the new project, and well, they need to get a welcome.

I was moved to one of the rooms where another team works on another kind of thing. It's a pretty calm place, the stretches of what I call "Productive Silence" are much longer than at my old chair. Because of this silence, the first day I was working in this room I heard a "pecking" on the wall where the air conditioner is located. At times, this noise would throw me out of focus and I would just stay there staring at that wall the pecking was coming from and thinking that at any moment, something was going to emerge from it, something like the air conditioner was going to "spark" or that it was simply going to stop working.

At some point, I asked my new coworkers about that pecking that could be heard so often. 


-*Guys, is that noise coming from the AC?*


-*Nah, man. That's the Woodpecker we got 'round here. It's always peckin' and tryin' to drill a hole in the wall.*

Time and again, the pecking threw me off a bit. It's undeniable that I'm a person who is easy to distract; anyone else wouldn't pay attention to it or simply wouldn't even notice that constant and repetitive "little noise" coming from this wall.

After a few days, sitting down after lunch, I heard once again the resounding noise of that bird trying without rest to find or achieve something with that wall. In a small part of my mind I could hear that thought, which I heard a little louder each time, saying...

-*I'm so sick of that bird...*

I didn't pay much attention to that stubborn and shallow thought; I started looking at that wall closely and decided to understand that bird's motivation. At a certain moment, I had an epiphany that abruptly changed the way I saw that bird, not as a nuisance but as a way to motivate myself.

- That bird works without rest, without excuses. It just does the work it has to do in order to achieve what it wants.

- With its little brain, that bird is capable of focusing on a single task, which is to achieve that blessed hole it longs so much to get.

- After its hard work, that bird knows it will have its rewards... be it that nest it wants so badly to finish building for its chicks, or that food it wants so badly to taste.

Having said and expressed these thoughts, I decided to apply the woodpecker's philosophy to myself... a philosophy I had already known for some time but that I had stopped following a bit...

- Don't get tired, don't give up until good is better and better is excellent.

- Small steps lead to success. Be consistent.

- The results you get will be directly proportional to the effort you put in.

**All growth depends on activity. There is no physical or intellectual development without applying the slightest effort and effort means...**
### **WORK**
