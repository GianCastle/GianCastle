When will I be able to reach it?; repeating my intention is not enough. Even if I take it in my hands, it disappears like an illusion.

Now I understand, it has all been useless and it didn't work. That thing about "tomorrow" has gone far from my sight.

I understand now, that it will end without my having a choice; and I will keep going in circles in the rain with my disappointment.

I wonder since when I've been like this. I have ignored this existence that was special like the "rainbow" and even if I look at myself in a mirror, I can't see through it; and by the time I realize it, I will be passing the time in solitude again.

I have been pierced by the lies I told myself yesterday and the things I dislike about myself have bloomed again.

I understand now, it is my own words that made me drown when I dove into a sea of the dead.

I understand now, there is no one else here. I will try to fly through a sky full of rainbows.

What I chase, is the future I drew one day, but I blamed someone else again.

Now I understand, that it will be useless this way
Will it be okay to believe in the “future me” that these eyes have discovered?

I understand now, I must say goodbye. I am heading to the other future and it waits for me beyond my illusion.
